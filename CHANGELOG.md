## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-20 · A change log, and updates that fit your role

- **Change log** _(New · Admins)_ — Configure → Change log lists every release and every change in order, with filters and downloads (CSV, Markdown, JSON) for your records.
- **What’s new, made for you** _(Improved · Everyone)_ — After an update, HatRack opens an illustrated summary of the changes that apply to you, with a Show me button on each. Help → What’s new shows it again.
- **Updates for your role** _(New · Everyone)_ — Choose Everyday use or Admin for this device. Everyday users see changes to daily work; admins also see setup changes. Applying an admin’s setup template switches a new team member to Everyday use.
- **The log can’t skip a change** _(New · Admins)_ — Publishing stops if the app changed without a new version and a change-log entry, and a CHANGELOG.md file in the repository is rebuilt from the same list.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-18 · Getting-started tour

- **Getting-started tour** _(New · Everyone)_ — Step-by-step slides with real screenshots — one track for everyday use, one for setting up. Opens on first launch; Help → Getting started tour any time.

- **Published automatically** _(New · Admins)_ — HatRack is hosted with GitHub Pages; every push rebuilds the app and its docs and republishes the site.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-30 · Subtasks, colors and the visual guide

- **Subtasks with drag-to-nest** _(New · Everyone)_ — Drop a card on the middle of another card — on the board or in the outline — to make it a subtask. Up to three levels.
- **Three-line previews** _(New · Everyone)_ — Long titles and notes fold away; Show more opens notes, subnotes and subtasks.
- **Card colors and right-click menu** _(New · Everyone)_ — Tint any card; right-click for color, subtasks, nesting and moving.
- **Drag in the outline** _(Improved · Everyone)_ — Reorder, move and nest tasks, and move subnotes between tasks.
- **Setup templates** _(New · Admins)_ — Share one structure with a team; Restore → Apply setup merges it by name.
- **Visual guide** _(New · Everyone)_ — An illustrated guide to every screen, menu and setting, opened from Help.
- **Home-screen icon** _(New · Everyone)_ — Add HatRack to a phone’s home screen; it gets its own icon.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-28 · First release

- **Quick add with shortcuts** _(New · Everyone)_ — Type a task and press Enter; @group, #type, !priority, ~time, ^date and *repeat fill in the details.
- **Import long lists** _(New · Everyone)_ — Paste notes or open a .txt, .md or .csv file. Headings become groups, keywords sort the rest, and repeats merge into one card.
- **Triage** _(New · Everyone)_ — Sort unsorted tasks one at a time, with one key press each.
- **Visual board** _(New · Everyone)_ — Drag cards between columns to move them, Alt-drag to copy, paste text to add; columns and rows can be any field.
- **List, Outline and Reports** _(New · Everyone)_ — A sortable table, a printable outline by group, and the numbers: hours, overdue, next 14 days.
- **Notes, subnotes and attachments** _(New · Everyone)_ — Each task holds notes, a subnote checklist, links and files kept in the browser.
- **Export to your task app** _(New · Everyone)_ — Todoist, Asana, spreadsheet CSV, Markdown, plain list, calendar (.ics) and HatRack files.
- **Configure the structure** _(New · Admins)_ — Parents, entities and sub-branches with auto-sort keywords; task types, priorities, statuses, durations and custom fields.
- **Backups and restore** _(New · Admins)_ — Download everything as a file; restore it by merging or replacing.
